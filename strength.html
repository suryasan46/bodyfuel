💪 Strength Training

Get Stronger, Leaner, Healthier

Strength training is a core part of overall fitness. It helps you build muscle, burn fat, and improve long-term health.

“Strong is not just about muscles — it's about discipline, consistency, and mindset.”

🔥 Why Strength Training Matters

  • Builds lean muscle → Boosts metabolism
  • Reduces body fat → Helps weight management
  • Prevents muscle loss with age
  • Improves balance & stability
  • Enhances mental health

Also helps manage:

  • Arthritis
  • Back pain
  • Diabetes
  • Heart disease

🏋️ Types of Strength Training

  • Bodyweight – Push-ups, squats
  • Resistance bands
  • Free weights
  • Machines
  • Suspension training

🚀 How to Get Started

  • Warm up 5–10 minutes
  • 12–15 reps per set
  • Increase weight gradually
  • Rest between workouts
  • Focus on form

⏱️ Training Frequency

Strength: 2x per week

Cardio: 150 min moderate or 75 min intense

📈 Results

With consistency, you’ll see results in just a few weeks. Strength builds gradually, but the long-term benefits are powerful.

✅ Key Takeaway

👉 Build muscle | Burn fat | Stay healthy for life
